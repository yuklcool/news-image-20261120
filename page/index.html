VICE CITY · DAY ONE

GTA VI SMASHES ALL RECORDS

An estimated 24.58 million copies — pre-orders included — and about $2.36bn in revenue on launch day, per Sensor Tower: the biggest entertainment launch ever. Three in four copies sold on PlayStation 5; the game scored 99 on Metacritic. Take-Two has yet to release its own figures.

THE HAGUE · WORLD COURT

MYANMAR DID COMMIT GENOCIDE, COURT RULES

The ICJ finds, 9–6, that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya — the first time the court has held a state responsible for genocide. Reparations ordered.

UKRAINE · OVERNIGHT

RUSSIA STRIKES KYIV’S BRIDGES

Eight killed, 28 injured as missiles hit three bridges over the Dnipro. Nine regions targeted in the overnight barrage — retaliation for Ukraine’s Crimea Bridge strikes.
